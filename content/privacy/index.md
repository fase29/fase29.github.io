## Visitor statistics

To see how many people read these sites and which pages they visit, I use
[GoatCounter](https://www.goatcounter.com/), an open-source analytics service. When a
page loads, a small script from `gc.zgo.at` sends GoatCounter the page address, the
referring page, your browser and operating system, your screen size, and the country
derived from your IP address.

GoatCounter sets no cookies and does not store your IP address or anything else in your
browser. To avoid counting the same visitor twice, it keeps your IP address and browser
details in memory only, linked to a random ID, for up to eight hours; they are never
written to its database. The statistics I see are aggregated and do not identify
individual visitors.

The legal basis is my legitimate interest in understanding how these sites are used
(Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). For details, see the
[GoatCounter privacy policy](https://www.goatcounter.com/help/privacy). If you block the
script, for example with a content blocker, the sites work exactly the same.
